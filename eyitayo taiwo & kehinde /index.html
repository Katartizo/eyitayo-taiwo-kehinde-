OUR SHARED JOURNEY

Two Lives. One Beautiful Story.

From the unexpected blessing of our arrival to the memories we continue to create, this is the story of two lives growing together.

Our parents had initially planned to have three children. But God had a different plan and blessed them with two more. On November 22, 2000, our story began.

We were told that Mum experienced sickness throughout her pregnancy. We are forever grateful for her sacrifice and for Dad's love and support.

A special shout-out to our amazing elder siblings: Olabisi, Akintayo and Tomilola. We are blessed to have you.

Our arrival also made history on Dad's side of the family, where twins had not previously been born. From Mum's side, twins were already part of the family story. Shout-out to our cousins Taiwo and Kehinde Ojo, and Ogundijo Michael and Emmanuel.

Our educational journey began at Victory Nursery and Primary School. We later transferred to New Name Nursery and Primary School because of the distance from home and changes in our family routine.

Those early years gave us memories that will always remain part of our story. There are many more childhood experiences to share, but some stories deserve another day!

We began secondary school in 2011 at Oliver Baptist High School, Oyo, and completed that chapter in 2017. From JSS 1 to JSS 3, we were in the same class.

But senior secondary school brought an unexpected twist. During class placement, I was assigned to Commercial while my twin sister was placed in Science.

I was so unhappy that I cried when I got home! My sister promised to speak to one of the teachers the following day.

The teacher eventually called me and asked, "Are you Taiwo?" I answered yes. She then said, "Your sister loves you so much, and I love twins." I couldn't help smiling.

About 90% of our teachers knew my sister because she was more outspoken. I was the quiet one, with just one friend at the time. Becoming Assistant Class Captain in SS pushed me to interact more. At least, I tried! 😂

I gained admission into the Federal University of Technology, Minna, in the 2019/2020 academic session before my twin sister.

I wasn't particularly happy about going ahead without her. I kept encouraging her to apply to the same university, and when she eventually gained admission, it became one of my happiest moments.

People had often asked where my twin sister was and why I gained admission before her. Having her join me meant we could share another important chapter of our lives.

Through every season, God has remained faithful. We have countless memories, experiences and stories still waiting to be told.

I love my twin sister so much. Even if I get married tomorrow, I will always love her. She will forever remain part of my story.

Two lives. One beginning. Countless memories. One unbreakable bond.

Our story is still being written.
